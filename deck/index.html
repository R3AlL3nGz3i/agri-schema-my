The Safety Stack Two layers · no REJECT ever ships

Two review gates every entry must pass.

Layer 1

Compliance gate

deterministic
  • Banned / restricted denylist with salt-form matching
  • MRL & PHI checks against limits
  • Required Bahasa Malaysia fields present
  • REJECT is a hard block — never embedded
Layer 2

Grounded review

retrieval-grounded
  • Every dosage / PHI / registration claim must cite a retrieved MARDI passage
  • Ungrounded claim → FLAG, not PASS
  • No matching passage → the claim does not ship
  • Passing entries carry their citations forward
Guarantee: no REJECT entry ever ships to a farmer.
